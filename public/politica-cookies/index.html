Cookie policy

Last updated: 01/04/2026. At present, nexobase.dev only uses technical or strictly necessary cookies for the basic operation, security and stability of the site.

Current technical-only mode No active analytics No active marketing

1. What cookies are

Cookies are files or devices downloaded to the user's terminal in order to store and retrieve information about browsing or about the use of the website.

2. What cookies this website currently uses

As of this policy, nexobase.dev only uses technical or strictly necessary cookies for operation, security, stability, load balancing, basic maintenance of the site and, where applicable, to remember indispensable technical preferences of the user.

These cookies do not require prior consent insofar as they are necessary for the transmission of a communication or to provide a service expressly requested by the user.

3. Non-essential cookies

At present, this website does not activate non-essential analytics, advertising or personalization cookies without the user's prior consent.

4. Possible future activation of analytics cookies

If analytics tools, including Google Analytics, are activated in the future, those cookies will remain blocked until the user provides explicit consent through the relevant consent management system.

  • A first information layer will be shown.
  • The user will be able to accept, reject or configure cookies.
  • No analytics cookies will be installed before consent.
  • The user will be able to withdraw consent as easily as it was given.
  • The cookie policy will be updated to identify new tools and purposes.

5. How to manage or disable cookies

Users may allow, block or delete cookies through their browser settings or, where applicable, from the cookie settings panel available on the website.

Disabling certain technical cookies may affect the proper functioning of the site.

6. Changes to the cookie policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when the cookies used, their purposes or the third parties involved change, or when legal or technical changes require it.
